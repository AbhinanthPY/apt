Before you contact us

Good to know.

Can I send my roof photos on WhatsApp?+

Yes. Roof photos and a recent electricity bill can help with an initial discussion. We will still need to assess the property before finalising a system design.

Does requesting a visit confirm an appointment?+

No. Your request shares your preferred date and time with Aptech. Our team will contact you to agree and confirm a suitable appointment.

Can you help with an existing system?+

Send the inverter or panel details, any visible error message and a description of the issue. Our team can discuss the next step after reviewing the information.
